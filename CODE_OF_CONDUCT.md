# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **Contributor Covenant 2.1**.

The full text of the covenant is published at:

  https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

By participating in this project (filing issues, submitting pull requests,
joining discussions) you agree to abide by its terms.

## Reporting

Reports of unacceptable behavior may be sent to the project maintainer:

- GitHub: `@cyhsieh817`

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will review and respond
within a reasonable timeframe.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces: the GitHub repository (issues, PRs,
discussions, code), any official chat channels, and any in-person or virtual
events organized in connection with the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may, at their discretion, take any action they deem
appropriate, including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans from
project spaces. Decisions follow the Enforcement Guidelines section of
Contributor Covenant 2.1.
